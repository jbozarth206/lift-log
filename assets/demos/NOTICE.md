# Exercise form illustrations

Lift Log bundles start and peak frames for the exercises in its built-in plans.

- **Source:** [Open Exercise Illustrations](https://github.com/hitmanrepo/open-exercise-illustrations)
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain)
- **What is included:** only the frames mapped to Lift Log exercises (resized WebP). The rest of that dataset is not redistributed here.
- **What they are:** consistent studio illustrations of one figure, start position and end position. The app crossfades those two frames so the movement reads in motion. They are a form guide, not coaching or medical advice.

No Gym Visual, ExerciseDB, or other copyrighted GIF libraries are used.
