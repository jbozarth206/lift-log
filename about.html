Lift Log

A personal workout, nutrition and health log. Web app (PWA), single user. Last updated November 2026.

What it does

Lift Log tracks workouts (sets, reps, weights), body weight and measurements, food, and personal health notes on the user's own phone. With the patient's permission it can read their laboratory results from an Epic MyChart account (SMART on FHIR, patient standalone launch) so they can see their bloodwork with reference ranges and trend charts next to their training log.

Data it reads from Epic

  • Patient.Read: the patient's name, only to confirm the right MyChart account is connected.
  • Observation.Search (Labs): laboratory results (value, unit, reference range, flag, date).
  • DiagnosticReport.Search (Results): lab report/panel names, used to group results by draw.

The app is read-only: it never writes anything to Epic or MyChart.

Where data is stored (privacy)

  • All data is stored in the browser storage (localStorage) on the user's own device.
  • MyChart access tokens are stored only on that device. They are never included in backups, never uploaded and never shared. The app is a public client with no client secret.
  • If the user turns on "Coach sync" in Settings, a copy of their log (including imported lab results, but never tokens) is uploaded to a private GitHub repository owned by the user, for their own personal coaching review. Nothing is sent to the app developer, advertisers or any other third party.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.

Control, revocation and terms

  • The user chooses whether to connect MyChart and can disconnect at any time in the app (Track › Labs › Disconnect), which deletes the tokens from the device. Access can also be revoked in MyChart (Account settings › Linked apps and devices).
  • Imported results can be deleted in the app; "Erase all data" in Settings deletes everything stored on the device.

Exercise form demos

Workout cards include a short looping form preview. The drawings are from Open Exercise Illustrations (CC0 1.0 public domain), cropped and saved in this app so they work offline. They show a start position and an end position of the same movement. They are a visual guide only, not coaching or medical advice. See assets/demos/NOTICE.md.

Not medical advice

Lift Log displays the user's own records. It does not diagnose, interpret or give medical or dosing advice. Always discuss results with a clinician.
